Your Privacy, Drawn Clearly

Privacy First

At Scribble, we believe your creativity is personal. Our app is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. This policy outlines our commitment to keeping your data exactly where it belongs: with you.

No Data Collection

Scribble does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data about you.

We do not track your usage, we do not require any account creation, and we do not have access to your sketches or any other information you create within the app.

Local Processing

Everything that happens in Scribble happens exclusively on your device. All sketches, settings, and interactions are processed and stored locally. No information is ever uploaded to a server or shared with third parties.

Zero Advertisements

Scribble is a tool for creation, not a platform for tracking. We do not include any advertisements, trackers, or marketing SDKs that could compromise your privacy.

Future Changes

If our privacy practices ever change, we will notify you directly within the app. However, our core philosophy of local-first privacy will always remain the foundation of Scribble.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please feel free to reach out to us through the app store contact information.
