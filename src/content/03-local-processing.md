Everything you create in Scribble stays exclusively on your device. Your sketches, personal settings, and local interactions are never uploaded to a server or shared. While anonymous diagnostic and analytic data is securely transmitted to help us maintain app stability (as detailed in our data collection section), your creative content remains private and in your control.
