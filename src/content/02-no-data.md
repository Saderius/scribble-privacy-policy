Scribble is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect personal data like your name, email, or account information, and **we never sell your data**. To ensure app stability and improve the user experience, we collect limited, anonymous diagnostic data including crash logs, approximate location, app interactions, and device identifiers. These identifiers may be used by diagnostic or future advertising SDKs solely for technical purposes like frequency capping and performance analysis. This data is handled securely by our service provider, Google Firebase, and collection remains optional via the app settings.
