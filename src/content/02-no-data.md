Scribble is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect personal data like your name, email, or account information, and **we never sell your data**. To ensure app stability and improve the user experience, we collect limited, anonymous diagnostic data including crash logs, approximate location, app interactions, and device identifiers. These identifiers are used by diagnostic and advertising SDKs (such as AdMob) for technical purposes like frequency capping and performance analysis. This data is handled securely by our service provider, Google, and collection remains optional via the app settings.
